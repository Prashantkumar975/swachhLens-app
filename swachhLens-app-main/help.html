Help Desk

Find solutions to common issues and get the help you need.

👤

Account Help

  • Login problems: Ensure your email and password are correct. Clear browser cache if needed.
  • Registration problems: Use a valid email address and password of at least 6 characters.
  • Forgot password: Currently, password reset is not available. Contact support for assistance.
  • Role confusion: Citizens report waste. Employees clean and verify. Choose the correct role during registration.
📸

Reporting Help

  • Cannot upload image: Check your browser permissions. Try uploading instead of using the camera.
  • Camera not working: Ensure camera permissions are granted in your browser settings. Try the "Upload Image" option as a fallback.
  • Location permission denied: Enable location access in your browser, or manually type the address in the location field.
  • Report not submitting: Ensure all required fields are filled: location, photo, and description.
📍

Tracking Help

  • Report stays pending: Reports wait for group lead approval before dispatch. This may take some time depending on your area.
  • No employee assigned: After dispatch approval, a crew member is assigned. Check the report detail for status updates.
  • Cleanup still pending: Crew members work through their queues. Your report will move to Verification once collected.
  • Status meanings: Pending → In Progress → Verification → Resolved. Each step has a responsible owner.
🧹

Employee Help

  • Cannot see tasks: Tasks appear after a group lead approves the AI dispatch. Check your assigned tasks section.
  • Cannot upload proof: Use the "Mark as Collected" button on your task card. Ensure you have a photo ready.
  • Verification pending: Your group lead reviews completed work. Check back for approval or rework feedback.
⚙️

Technical Help

  • Website not loading: Check your internet connection. Try clearing browser cache or using a different browser.
  • API errors: The backend server may be temporarily unavailable. Try again in a few minutes.
  • Browser compatibility: SwachhLens works best on modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
  • Dark mode issues: Toggle dark mode using the moon/sun icon in the navigation bar.
📩
