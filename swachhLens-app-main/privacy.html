Privacy Policy

How SwachhLens handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and role selection (Citizen or Employee). This information is used to identify you and provide appropriate access to platform features.

Waste Report Data

When you submit a waste report, we collect the photo, location data, description, and any additional information you provide. This data is used to dispatch and track cleaning operations.

Uploaded Photos

Photos uploaded to SwachhLens are stored to enable AI waste analysis, crew dispatch, and cleanup verification. Photos are retained as part of the report record.

Location Data

Location information is collected to route waste reports to the correct municipal area and dispatch appropriate cleaning crews. Location data is used solely for operational purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the SwachhLens platform
  • To dispatch waste reports to appropriate cleaning crews
  • To enable AI-powered waste classification and analysis
  • To track report status and cleanup verification
  • To display community leaderboards and statistics
  • To communicate account-related information

3. AI Analysis

Uploaded photos are analyzed by AI to classify waste types and severity. This analysis is performed automatically and assists in prioritizing dispatches. AI analysis results are stored alongside report data.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on our servers and protected by standard security measures. We use JWT-based authentication to secure API access. However, as a demo project, we cannot guarantee the same level of security as production enterprise systems.

5. Data Retention

Account information and report data are retained as long as your account is active. Report data, including photos, is retained as part of the cleanup verification record. You may request account deletion by contacting support@swachhlens.app.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Report data is shared internally between citizens, cleaning crews, and group leads to facilitate the waste management workflow.

7. User Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for all activity that occurs under your account. You should not share your login information with others.

8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us at support@swachhlens.app or visit our Contact page.
