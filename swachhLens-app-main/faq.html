Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SwachhLens.

General

SwachhLens is an AI-powered civic waste reporting and cleanup verification platform. Citizens report waste with a photo, AI classifies and prioritizes the issue, municipal crews are dispatched, and group leads verify the cleanup — creating a complete accountability loop.

Log in as a Citizen, tap "Report Waste," take or upload a photo of the waste, add a location and description, and submit. The AI will analyze your photo and route it to the appropriate cleaning crew.

When you upload a photo, SwachhLens uses vision AI to analyze the image, classify the waste type (e.g., overflow, illegal dump, construction debris), and estimate severity. This helps prioritize which reports need immediate attention.

No. Citizen and Employee accounts serve different roles. Citizens report and track waste. Employees (cleaning crew members) receive and complete cleanup tasks. You choose your role during registration.

Employees are organized into area groups. Each group has a group lead who approves AI-suggested dispatches and verifies completed work. This ensures accountability — no task is marked resolved without human verification.

Reporting & Tracking

Go to your Citizen Dashboard and check "My Reports." Each report shows its current status: Pending → In Progress → Verification → Resolved. You can filter by status using the pill buttons.

After a crew member marks a task as collected, it moves to the Verification stage. The group lead reviews the proof of work and either confirms it (marking it Resolved) or sends it back for rework.

Yes. When submitting a report, you can choose "Upload Image" to select an existing photo from your device instead of using the camera.

Your report enters the AI prioritization pipeline. The system classifies the waste, assigns a severity level, and suggests the appropriate area group and crew. A group lead reviews and approves the dispatch, and a crew member is assigned to clear the waste.

Your report is routed to the municipal area group responsible for your location. The AI suggests which group and crew member should handle it, but a group lead makes the final dispatch decision.
