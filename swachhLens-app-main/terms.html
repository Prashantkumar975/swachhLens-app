2. About SwachhLens

SwachhLens is an AI-powered civic waste reporting and cleanup verification platform. It is currently a demo/student project designed to showcase how technology can improve urban waste management. It is not an official government service.

3. User Accounts

Citizen Accounts

Citizen accounts allow users to report waste issues, upload photos, track report status, and view community leaderboards. Citizens are responsible for providing accurate information when submitting reports.

Employee Accounts

Employee accounts are for cleaning crew members and group leads. Employees receive assigned tasks, upload proof of completed work, and verify cleanups. Employees must perform their duties responsibly and accurately.

4. Waste Report Submissions

When submitting waste reports, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate location information
  • Upload genuine photos of actual waste issues
  • Provide honest descriptions of the problem
  • Not submit duplicate or fraudulent reports
  • Not use the platform for purposes unrelated to waste management

5. Photo Uploads

By uploading photos to SwachhLens, you grant the platform permission to store and analyze these images for waste classification purposes. Photos should depict actual waste situations and must not contain inappropriate, offensive, or illegal content.

6. Location Information

SwachhLens uses your location to route waste reports to the appropriate municipal area. Location data is used solely for dispatching cleaning crews and is not shared with third parties.

7. AI Analysis

SwachhLens uses artificial intelligence to analyze uploaded photos and classify waste types. AI analysis is provided as an assistive tool and may not always be accurate. Final dispatch decisions are made by human group leads.
